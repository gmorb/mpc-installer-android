Connect to your MPC

Your MPC needs root SSH access (a modded unit). Find its IP address in the MPC's network settings or in your router.

Use at your own risk. Plugins are community-made and run as root on your device. They can crash MPC or lose unsaved work, and at worst the device may need its OS reinstalled. Save your project and back up first. Not affiliated with Akai Professional.

      Use the MPC's internal drive instead (not recommended)

      The internal drive is the MPC's built-in flash storage (eMMC). Installing and updating plugins, and the files plugins write, all add wear to it, and if it fails the MPC itself needs repair. An SD card, USB drive or SSD is cheap to replace: use one of those whenever you can.

      Remove plugins

      The plugins on your MPC. Removing deletes the plugin's folder and its entry in MPC's plugin list (MPC's settings are backed up first). Your own files inside it, such as ROMs, kits and dumps, are kept. Addins (libraries MPC loads when it starts) are listed last: removing one runs its own uninstaller, which takes it out of MPC's start-up list and deletes its folder, settings included.

      Something missing from the plugin list?
      Show      
  
      Installing already registers its plugins. Use this only if one is missing: a plugin folder only shows up in MPC once it is in MPC's plugin list. Folders you copied in yourself, or that MPC forgot (for example because a card was out when MPC started), are not. This adds them, and drops entries whose plugin file is gone. Everything else in the list is left alone. MPC's settings are backed up first and MPC restarts once.

      Clean up old backups
      Show      
  
      Every install, removal and sync leaves a copy of MPC's settings file on the device. They are small, but they pile up. This keeps the newest ones and deletes the older ones. The newest backup is never deleted, and MPC is not restarted.
